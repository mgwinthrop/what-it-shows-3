#4471 · what the merge held: three problems, not one

owner support desk living reviewed 2026-10-31 · 2 days ago

#4471 says one issue and one fix. The 412 reports merged into it are not one issue. Read against the tenant directory (locale and booking form) and the report bodies, they hold three problems. Only the first is the parser bug the ticket describes.

The parser fix will not stop problems 2 and 3. They have no ticket of their own until the tickets below are filed.

The three problems

374 + 33 + 5 = 412. Every report is counted once. The export holds 427 rows because its pages overlap; 15 reports appear twice and are counted once.

How this differs from the desk’s cause table. The cause table counts by tenant and channel only, so it shows two causes: parser 379 and hint 33. It files the 5 one-day reports under the parser and footnotes them (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620; the desk lists them in its own notes). Their bodies describe a day shifting, not a month flipping: the same meeting giving two cities two different days, an invite arriving before the booking existed, a Tuesday showing as Monday. Counted apart, the parser group is 374, not 379. The five differ from each other, so treat their shared cause as a hypothesis until engineering reproduces it.

How to tell them apart

  • Look up the tenant, not the report. Every tenant in the directory writes day first. Whether the cause is the parser or the form hint depends on which booking form the tenant runs and how the report arrived. Only a report that came through a booking-v3 form is problem 2. The parser flips every other day-first report.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess. It is made from the reporter’s browser. 23 reports it tagged as hint are the parser bug, and 15 it tagged as parser are the form hint.
  • Read the body for problem 3. A month flip changes the month. These change only the day.

Why the merge hid this

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject line alone. The subjects here are things like “Wrong day on the invite”, which fit all three problems. Merging on subject is fast, but it counts three bugs once and hides two of them. The reports were merged into #4471 months before anyone read the bodies; the oldest merge in the export is dated 2026-07-02.

What to do

  1. File one ticket per problem (the tickets for this page are in the repository’s issues).
  2. Move the problem 2 and problem 3 reports out of #4471, so the parser fix closes only what it fixes.
  3. Reopen the question in decision 0001: when two reports share a subject, check the tenant’s locale and booking form before merging. A new record replaces 0001; 0001 is not edited.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is mostly one bug reported many times, until it isn’t. Count the causes before you merge, not after.
  • A number that can be computed should be computed. These counts come from analysis/count.mjs, which reads the export and prints them; re-run it when the export changes.
